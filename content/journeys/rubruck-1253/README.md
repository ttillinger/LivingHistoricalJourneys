# rubruck-1253

The journey of the Franciscan friar William of Rubruck to the court of the Great Khan
Möngke: Constantinople (7 May 1253) to Tripoli in Syria (15 August 1255). Built in
November 2026 (ROADMAP R3) in place of the proof of concept's invented "Silk Road
caravan", which had nothing to source.

**Source of truth:** [`authoring.json`](./authoring.json). `journey.json`, `bundle.json`
and `routes/*.geojson` are generated by `pnpm content:build`.

## What is here (version 1)

- **Friar William** track: 96 dated stops and about 40 undated waypoints. No roads:
  straight lines between points. A waypoint whose place *and* day are both estimates
  is undated, so the marker keeps an even pace between the points he dates himself.
- One stat: the size of his party (a step at each change).
- 102 moments, 37 with a line from Rockhill's translation of 1900; 28 timed
  episodes; 35 conditions, each with a camera `zoom`.
- `labels.json`: the names Rubruck used (Etilia for the Volga, Caracarum, Turkie).

## Editing rules

Same as Napoleon (see its README). Dates are **Julian**. Do not quote Jackson's
translation of 1990: it is in copyright.

## research/

Two research files (outbound to 4 January 1254; from 5 January 1254). Each row says
whether its date is stated, feast-dated, counted or estimated. Read `corrections` and
`openQuestions` before changing a date or a place.

Known soft spots:

- One witness. Nothing here can be checked against a second account.
- Almost every steppe waypoint, both ways, is interpolated along Rockhill's
  reconstruction. The Don and Ural crossings, Batu's camp, Baiju's camp and the
  Köse Dağ battlefield are estimates.
- Möngke's winter camp is placed on the upper Ongi from the Yuan shi; one modern
  writer puts it much further south-east.
- Kinchat to Cailac in ten days is about 800 km, which is why Yule doubted the
  "twelve days" of rest at Cailac. The Ili/Chu and Equius questions are open.
- Jackson and Morgan's edition was seen only through readings reported in the
  University of Washington text; Yule and Pelliot only at second hand.
- Head-counts on the way home are approximate.
- Two moments the research placed between Antioch and Tripoli on guessed dates were
  dropped; that stretch has no moment for 47 days.
- The 13 April 1253 sermon in Hagia Sophia is researched but falls before the start.
