# shackleton-1914

Shackleton's Imperial Trans-Antarctic Expedition, Weddell Sea party: Buenos Aires
(26 October 1914) to Punta Arenas (3 September 1916). Rebuilt to the Napoleon standard
in November 2026 (ROADMAP R3).

**Source of truth:** [`authoring.json`](./authoring.json). `journey.json`, `bundle.json`
and `routes/*.geojson` are generated by `pnpm content:build`.

## What is here (version 2)

- **Shackleton** track: about 300 dated positions. The ship to the wreck, the floe
  camps, the three boats, the James Caird, the crossing of South Georgia, the four
  rescue ships. No roads: every leg is a straight line between two fixes, with a few
  undated points to keep ships off the land.
- **The men on Elephant Island** track: exists only from 24 April to 30 August 1916
  (`appears` / `leaves`); the viewer hides it outside those dates.
- Stats: men with Shackleton (a step at each parting), sledge dogs and temperature
  (both `lapses`: the stat disappears when the record ends).
- 118 moments, 27 with a line from *South*; 32 timed episodes; 41 conditions, each
  with a camera `zoom` (open sea 4.4, the drift 6.6, the island crossing 10.5).
- `labels.json`: period names. "Endurance lost" sits at the wreck's found position.

## Editing rules

Same as Napoleon (see its README): UTC written from local mean time, a source for
every fact, bump `version` on change. West longitudes are negative, so local noon is
*later* than 12:00Z (at 52°W, 15:28Z).

## research/

Two research files (to the sinking; from the sinking to the rescue). Each ends with
`corrections` and `openQuestions`. Read those before changing a date or a position.

Known soft spots:

- Wordie's daily positions for January–September 1915 were read from an OCR scan;
  five are flagged, and two rows were dropped as misreads.
- Boat positions on 10, 11 and 14 April 1916, and the turn-back points of the
  Southern Sky and the Instituto de Pesca, are estimates.
- Ocean Camp's first position (1 November 1915) is an estimate.
- The Yelcho's Ushuaia call is read as 26 August; the digitised log says the 25th at
  an impossible hour. Shackleton's passage from Port Stanley to Punta Arenas is
  dated 1–4 July by placeholder.
- Ship courses through the Strait of Magellan, the Beagle Channel and round South
  Georgia are schematic.
- The voyage out from Plymouth (8 August 1914) is researched but not shown: the
  marker follows Shackleton, who joined at Buenos Aires.
- Orde-Lees's bicycle is left out: no source dates it.
- No temperatures for Elephant Island; dog numbers stop in January 1916.
- The Ross Sea party is not covered at all.

`poc-seed.json` is the original proof-of-concept data, kept for provenance.
